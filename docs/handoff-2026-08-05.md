## What I decided, and why

Nine reds. Each was a decision, and each is recorded at the source as well as
here.

1. **Four new keywords** — `Equip`, `Weaponmaster`, `Quick-Draw`, `Repeat` —
   added to `KEYWORDS` **and** to `UNIMPLEMENTED_KEYWORDS` in the same change,
   so the 58 cards printing them report NOT implemented instead of reporting
   done and doing nothing. That map was kept empty-but-alive when `[Deflect]`
   left it, and its own comment predicted this reopening.
2. **`KW_PATTERN` widened to allow a hyphen.** It could not match `[Quick-Draw]`
   at all, so the keyword parsed onto no card and its 6 Gear would have read in
   play as simply not working. The bracket sweep caught it regardless, because
   it deliberately scans a wider grammar than the parser it guards — which is
   the whole reason those two differ, and it worked exactly as designed.
3. **`[Unique]` is a deckbuilding restriction, not an ability**, so it is
   allow-listed in `NON_KEYWORD_BRACKETS` — and `deck-validation.isUniqueCard`
   now actually enforces it (cap 1, not `MAX_COPIES`). Allow-listing without
   enforcing would have made the sweep pass on a bracket that still does
   nothing, which is the one outcome that gate exists to prevent.
4. **All 14 SFD dual-domain cards got `POWER_DOMAIN_ALT_OVERRIDES` entries.**
   Confirmed by pulling the art off the CMS url already in the JSON and reading
   the pip at 4x against a single-domain control. See the trap below.
5. **`isEligibleChampion` now folds the name prefix.** Rek'sai was undeckable —
   see below.
6. **HTML entities decoded in the loader**, not in the JSON.
7. **The battlefield gate scoped to `COMPLETE_SETS`**, like the card gates
   already were. OGN's 24 stay hard-gated; SFD's 15 report `0/15` as progress.
   A gate expected to be red says nothing when it is.
8. **Two synthetic fixtures moved off `SFD`**, because the pool absorbed their
   subjects: `set-coverage`'s `SFD-001` became a real card (Against the Odds),
   and the bracket sweep's positive control used `[Weaponmaster]`, which is now
   a declared keyword. A control whose subject the pool has absorbed proves
   nothing.
9. **The `shouldSkip` census now includes SFD.** It read OGN+OGS only, which
   made it blind to precisely the event it exists for — a new set stepping
   outside the markers — and it passed green on the day Spiritforged landed
   while measuring a pool that did not contain it.

Two deck fixtures changed premise rather than breaking: a real community export
had four "later-set" names that turned out to be Spiritforged's, and the `.deck`
file that was rejected for referencing SFD-185 is now rejected for UNL-128.

## Three findings worth more than the commit that carried them

**The prompt is wrong about XP, and it costs a whole subsystem.** `[Hunt]`,
`[Level]`, `[Ambush]` and `[Backline]` are printed by **zero** SFD cards — they
are Unleashed's. Measured over `sfd.json`, and also stated outright in two
places nobody had to measure: `model/keyword.ts`'s own doc comment and the
oracle's `Keyword.java`. **Rule 728's XP resource is not SFD work**, and neither
is the `[Ambush]` reinforce-by-presence question the prompt flags as possibly
already divergent. What the prompt did *not* predict is **`[Repeat]`**, which is
a second subsystem, 15 cards.

**Rek'sai - Void Burrower was undeckable, and only one test could see it.** She
was the only Legend in the pool with no eligible champion: the data cases her
name `Rek'sai` while both her champions are `Rek'Sai - Breacher` and `Rek'Sai -
Swarm Queen`, and the prefix match was literal. Since a legal deck must contain
a champion eligible for its Legend, **no legal deck existed for her at all** —
and nothing else in the repo would have reported it, because coverage counts
implementations, not deckability. Fixed by folding through the pool's existing
`foldCardName` rather than a second hand-rolled fold, which is the mistake the
decklist collision check already made once.

**A split pip's cost is the glyph count, not the colour count**, and I read it
wrong the first time. At low zoom the 2-Power dual-domain cards look like *two
separate pips, one per domain*; at 4x they are plainly **one capsule halved into
two colours with two glyphs inside it**, identical in kind to Tibbers. Without
the overrides, `lowestOrdinalDomain` demands the whole cost in one domain — so
Forgefire Cape, whose pip is half Calm and half Mind, could not be paid with a
Mind rune at all.

## The one thing I found and deliberately did not fix

**OGN's ten dual-domain Signature spells and OGS-024 look like split pips too.**

Pulling those cards' art as *controls* for the SFD check showed every one of
them with the same left/right two-colour capsule that defines a hybrid pip here.
That directly contradicts `card-loader`'s own note, which says Decisive Strike's
"pip is a solid single color and is NOT hybrid".

If the reading is right, **eleven cards in two declared-complete sets** charge
their whole Power cost in one domain while half their pip says otherwise — the
same defect the SFD entries just fixed.

Not acted on, on purpose: it re-costs cards across OGN and OGS, moves every
probe that plays them, and is not SFD work. It wants its own change, its own
before/after probe run, and a second person looking at the art. Held open in
three places so it cannot be lost — an `it.todo` in `test/card-loader.test.ts`,
a comment on the test that pins the current behaviour, and **Unverified #1** in
`docs/rules-conformance.md`.

## The deck generator, and the number that had been zero

`decks/deck-generator.ts` is new, and it closes the gap this handoff kept
naming. SFD reached 60 implemented cards while `exercised` reported **zero** of
them, because a card no deck contains cannot be played however many games run.

`generateCoveringDecks("SFD", registry)` deals every implemented card of a set a
seat, then builds one deck per Legend: **12 decks, 60 of 60 subjects seated, 0
orphans, 0 unbuildable.** Rek'sai builds, which proves her name-casing fix end to
end rather than only in a unit test.

    DECKS=sfd GAMES=40 npx tsx packages/engine/probes/exercised.ts
    -> SFD inDecks 79, exercised 63, invalid 0

`DECKS=<set>` is OPT-IN and the presets are untouched, deliberately: four probes
read `PRESET_DECKS` and three pin recorded figures, so changing what everyone
plays to measure one set would move numbers that exist to catch regressions.

**Two things it got wrong first, both caught by its own tests, and both worth
knowing before the next set.** Sorting the fill by id alone let `OGN-*` take all
40 slots — twelve SFD decks held three implemented SFD cards between them, and
every deck validated perfectly, because a deck of legal filler is legal. And
sorting still left 13 of 60 homeless, since 40 slots at 3 copies reach about 14
distinct cards; a sort is not a plan, so subjects are now DEALT and passed as
`priority`, where one that cannot be seated throws by name.

**It also turned a probe control red, correctly.** `activatedSeen` failed on 40
SFD games with zero activations — because the only activatable SFD card in the
pool is the Gold token, whose ability is `banksResource` and is dropped from the
AI's candidates on purpose. The control now checks its premise (does any deck
hold an ability this AI would take?) and reports `signals.activatableInDecks`
beside it, because a bare `true` cannot be told from "activations happened". It
reads 5 on the presets and 0 on SFD.
